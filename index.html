    Canadian Airspace Classes (A–G)
    • Class A / B: High-level / low-level controlled — ATC clearance required.
    • Class C / D: Major & regional airport Control Zones — NAV CANADA RPAS Auth required.
    • Class E: Controlled airspace (airways/control areas) — Auth required if entering.
    • Class F (CYR / CYA): CYR = Restricted (prohibited without permission, incl. microdrones); CYA = Advisory.
    • Class G: Uncontrolled airspace — Basic & Advanced operations permitted within Part IX limits.
    VHF Radio Callout Script (ROC-A Required)
    1. Who you’re calling → 2. Who you are → 3. Position → 4. Altitude AGL → 5. Intentions
    “[Aerodrome] Traffic, RPAS [Reg], 2 miles east of the field, operating surface to 300 feet AGL for the next 15 minutes, monitoring [Freq].”
    • Distress / Urgency: MAYDAY (immediate danger) • PAN-PAN (urgent safety).
    • Key Frequencies: 121.5 MHz Emergency Guard • 126.7 MHz Enroute Advisory.
    • Circuit: Standard Left-Hand traffic pattern unless CFS specifies right-hand.
    IMSAFE Fitness & 30-Second VO Brief
    Illness • Medication • Stress • Alcohol (12 h CARs rule) • Fatigue • Emotion / Eating
    • VO Comms & Sector: Confirm direct voice/radio link and assign airspace + bystander scan sectors.
    • Failsafe & ELZ: Brief RTH height (above tallest obstacle) and point out Emergency Landing Zone.
    • Callouts: “Launching”, “Manned aircraft [clock/alt]”, “Bystander entering”, “Lost link / RTH”.
    Mandatory Docs to Carry On Site (CARs Part IX)
    • Pilot Certificate: Basic or Advanced RPAS certificate (digital or printed) + Gov’t Photo ID.
    • Registration: Certificate of Registration + registration number visibly marked on drone (≥ 250 g).
    • Manuals (CAR 901.30): Manufacturer operating manuals immediately available to crew.
    • Authorizations: Active NAV CANADA RPAS Flight Authorization or SFOC-RPAS (if applicable).
    Hard Numbers & Mandatory Reporting
    • Ceiling (CAR 901.25): 400 ft (122 m) AGL, or +100 ft above a structure within 200 ft horizontally.
    • Aerodromes (CAR 901.47): 5.6 km (3 NM) airport • 1.9 km (1 NM) heliport • 5.6 km military (DND).
    • Recency (CAR 901.65): Qualifying activity required every 24 months.
    • Universal Rules: CAR 900.06 (no reckless ops, incl. <250 g) • CAR 900.08 (no emergency perimeters).
    • Reporting: Controlled/CYR flyaway → ATC immediately • Serious injury or manned collision → TSB Canada.
    Quick Mental Math & Field Rules of Thumb
    • Wind: 1 kt = 1.852 km/h • 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h.
    • Altitude: ASL = Ground Elevation + AGL • MEF = highest quadrant obstacle (ft ASL + buffer).
    • Magnetic Variation: East is Least (subtract East), West is Best (add West).
    • Fog / Icing Risk: Temp–Dewpoint spread ≤ 2–3 °C = high fog, mist, or rotor icing risk.
    • Cold LiPo: Pre-warm flight packs; expect higher internal resistance & voltage sag under load.
