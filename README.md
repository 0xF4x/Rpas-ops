A 100% offline-first, zero-dependency Progressive Web App (PWA) built for Canadian RPAS pilots operating under Transport Canada CARs Part IX.

## Highlights
- **Weight-Class Pre-Flight Checklist + Custom Gear Checks:** Adapts automatically for Micro (< 250 g), Small (250 g–25 kg), and Medium (> 25–150 kg) operations (`922.04`, `922.05`, `922.06`, Sheltered, EVLOS), plus persistent custom gear checks and a Live Flight Timer → Logbook bridge.
- **Solar / Civil Twilight, Live Kp Index & Live Wind:** Offline sunrise/sunset/civil twilight calculator (CAR 901.39 strobe check), live NOAA Kp geomagnetic index check, Open-Meteo live wind fetch, and an offline METAR decoder.
- **Canadian Airspace Launcher & CAR 901.27 Print/PDF Survey:** One-tap GPS lock + clipboard copy, direct links to NAV Drone & NRC Airspace Map, and a clean one-page printable Site Survey & Pre-Flight Record.
- **Fleet Presets, Individual Battery Inventory & 24-Month Recency (CAR 901.65):** One-click Canadian drone presets, per-battery cycle & storage-charge tracking, and recency expiry countdown.
- **Offline Canadian Airspace & VNC Cheat Sheet:** Pinned modal for Class A–G airspace, VNC/VTA symbols, METAR codes, and the ICAO phonetic alphabet.
- **Full 50-Question TP 15263E Exam, Readiness Trend & TP 15395E Flight Review Prep:** Quick 10, Full 50-Question / 60-Minute Mock Exam with domain breakdown + score trend chart, and an interactive Flight Review checklist.

## Disclaimer
Educational and pre-flight aid only. Always verify official airspace, NOTAMs, and current regulations with Transport Canada and NAV CANADA before launch.
