# RPAS Ops — Canadian Drone Companion

A 100% offline-ready, zero-dependency Progressive Web App (PWA) for Canadian RPAS pilots operating under Transport Canada CARs Part IX.

## Highlights
- **Weight-Class Pre-Flight Checklist:** Adapts automatically for Micro (< 250 g), Small (250 g–25 kg), and Medium (> 25–150 kg) operations (`922.04`, `922.05`, `922.06`, Sheltered, EVLOS).
- **Live Flight Timer → Logbook Bridge:** Time flights on the Checklist tab and pre-fill your Logbook in one tap.
- **Offline METAR Decoder & Wind Calculator:** Paste any raw METAR to decode wind/gusts (auto-applied in km/h), visibility, ceiling, temp/dewpoint, and altimeter.
- **Canadian Airspace Launcher & GPS:** One-tap GPS lock + clipboard copy with direct links to NAV Drone Viewer, NAV Drone Portal, and the NRC Drone Site Selection Tool.
- **CAR 901.27 Print / PDF Site Survey:** Generate a clean one-page printable Site Survey & Pre-Flight Record.
- **Fleet Presets, Battery Cycles & 24-Month Recency (CAR 901.65):** One-click Canadian drone presets, per-aircraft cycle counter, and recency expiry countdown.
- **TP 15263E Exam Prep:** 25 realistic Small Advanced questions across Quick 10 (instant feedback) and timed Practice Exam (30:00 timer, 1–25 question navigator, flag for review, and domain score breakdown).
- **Privacy-First Storage:** All data stays locally in `localStorage` with CSV flight log export, JSON backup/restore, and one-click clear.

## Disclaimer
Educational and pre-flight aid only. Always verify official airspace, NOTAMs, and current regulations with Transport Canada and NAV CANADA before launch.
